This chapter studies D. Stull's optimal oracles: Hausdorff and packing oracles that additionally resist being improved by extra information. The notion is defined through Kolmogorov complexity, yet it controls classical statements such as Marstrand's projection theorem, and whether a set has optimal oracles can depend on set-theoretic axioms. We use it as a case study in how algorithmic and classical methods interoperate.

- [Optimal Oracles via Kolmogorov Complexity](#section:Optimal_Oracles_via_Kolmogorov_Complexity): Stull's definitions in the language of algorithmic information theory, their equivalent full-dimensional forms, what is known about which sets have optimal oracles, and the split of the definition into a witness condition and a fitness condition.
