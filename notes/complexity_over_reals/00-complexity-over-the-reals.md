---
title: "Algorithmic Complexity over the Reals"
label: chapter:complexity_over_reals
---

This chapter lifts algorithmic complexity from finitary objects to infinitary ones (like points of Euclidean space) through their finitary approximations, and uses the lift to study effective dimension.

- [Lifting Prefix Complexity to Reals](#section:Lifting_Prefix_Complexity_to_Reals): the standard lift of prefix complexity to $\mathbb{R}^m$.
- [Effective Hausdorff Dimension](#section:Effective_Hausdorff_Dimension): constructive dimension and its characterizations by complexity, supergales, and tests.
- [Effective Dimension Variants](#section:Conditional_and_Mutual_Dimensions): mutual and conditional dimension.
- [Point-to-Set Principles](#section:PTS): characterizing the Hausdorff dimension and measure of a set by the effective dimensions of its points.
- [Robustness of the Lift](#section:Robustness_of_the_Lift): showing that the order of optimizers in the definition of conditional prefix complexity makes no difference up to sub-linear factors, and a conditional symmetry of information result.
- [Effective Dimension under Uniformly Continuous Maps](#section:Effective_Dimension_under_Uniformly_Continuous_Maps): confirming a modulus processing inequality for conditional prefix complexity under computable, uniformly continuous maps; concluding conditional dimension is invariant under bi-computable, bi-Lipschitz continuous maps.
